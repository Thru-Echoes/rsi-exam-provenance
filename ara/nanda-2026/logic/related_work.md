# Related work map

This is a routing map, not a finished literature review. Every external statement requires a checked primary citation before submission.

## RW01 — Self-improving-agent evaluation

**Type:** benchmark and evaluation protocol

**Delta:** This work treats decision-level auditability as separable from endpoint performance and reports modified-program, reduced-window evidence without presenting it as an official benchmark result.

## RW02 — Experiment tracking and artifact lineage

**Type:** experiment management and data lineage

**Delta:** The target is not only run metadata: it is an exact candidate-parent decision, its evidence roles, a distinct statistical verdict and operational disposition, and offline rule recomputation.

## RW03 — Agent traces and decision provenance

**Type:** execution traces and typed decision records

**Delta:** TRACE expresses who proposed and resolved a decision, while the profile verifier checks the bound files. The layers are complementary and neither inherits trust from the other.

## RW04 — Reproducible research artifacts

**Type:** executable papers and artifact evaluation

**Delta:** The ARA package preserves supported, refuted, and hypothesis claim states and binds them to an explicit evidence-reconciliation gate rather than presenting only successful outcomes.

## RW05 — Governance of agent-produced knowledge

**Type:** human review and knowledge governance

**Delta:** ProofPress appears only as a downstream import and human-review boundary. It does not replace source verification or turn imported evidence into an approved claim.
